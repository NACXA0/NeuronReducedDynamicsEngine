v0.1 is systems integration: AdExp-class F with a reportable ODE error, Shiu `W_syn = 0.275 mV` α start, sparse online propagation, optional FlyGym v2 plugin as an `EmbodiedEnv` backend.

Public wording: **types may bind different F**. Not “N reconstructed cell types” and not meaningful walking.
